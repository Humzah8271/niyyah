About Niyyah

Thoughtfully prepared
for your journey.

Care, clarity and useful essentials to help you approach Umrah feeling more organised, reassured and ready.

Our intention

A little less to work out.
More room to prepare.

I’m Humzah, the person behind Niyyah, a small business in Glasgow. I’m developing Niyyah to bring useful Umrah essentials and clear guidance together, so preparation feels simpler and more manageable.

The idea is a thoughtfully organised bag, with an explanation of what each item is for and how to use it. A personal welcome, readable instructions and useful contents should help you feel that someone has considered your journey carefully.

Printable planners and personalised gifts are available now through our Etsy shop. Our first physical kit, the Brothers’ Kit, is still being developed. Sisters’, couples’, family and group options are future plans.

نِيَّة
Intention at the centre

Niyyah means intention. It reminds us to keep the purpose of the journey in view, from the information we share to the welcome we hope to place inside every kit.

Faith shapes how we work: with care, honesty and respect for the people preparing for this journey.

What matters to us

Care in the details.

01

Help before a sale

Our guidance and educational videos should be useful in their own right. Learn and prepare at your own pace; the shop is there when you need it.

02

Clarity brings reassurance

Clear contents, readable instructions and straightforward explanations of what to bring yourself. We aim to ease preparation, without promising a journey free of every difficulty.

03

Useful by design

Every item should earn its place. We’re working towards practical, comfortable essentials and a bag that stays useful after you unpack.

04

Fair, clear choices

Our planned Essentials, Comfort and Luxury options will explain each extra and upgrade. Clear basic guidance belongs in every tier.

05

A personal welcome

Thoughtful organisation, a sincere thank-you and care in the small details. We want you to understand what you have and feel more prepared to use it.

06

Religious care

We support preparation; we are not a religious authority. Our instructional guide is being prepared for qualified review before sale. That review is not yet complete, and we do not claim scholar approval for our products. Where views differ, seek qualified guidance.

Preparation, with honest expectations.

Our kits are still in development. We’ll explain their final contents, prices and availability when they are ready.

Niyyah provides preparation products and information. Flights, accommodation, visas, personal documents, medicines and your individual needs remain separate. We want to help you pack with confidence, while being clear about what a kit can and cannot cover.

We use tools including AI to help with design and writing, and check the finished work. Current personalised products are made to order through Printify; each listing explains what you are buying.
