About Niyyah

Niyyah is a small business based in Glasgow, helping people across the UK prepare for Umrah and Hajj.

The story

Preparing for Umrah should feel a little easier.

I'm Humzah, the person behind Niyyah. I started the business in Glasgow in 2026 to bring useful Umrah essentials and clear preparation resources together in one place.

You can currently buy printable planners and personalised gifts through our Etsy shop. I’m also working on Umrah kits and a guide that will be reviewed by a qualified scholar before it goes on sale.

I run Niyyah myself, using tools including AI to help with design and writing, then checking the finished work. Our personalised items are made to order by UK printers through Printify.

نِيَّة
Why "Niyyah"

Niyyah means intention. Every act of worship begins with one, and so does every Umrah: you make your intention before you enter ihram.

إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ

“Actions are judged by intentions.”

Sahih al-Bukhari 1
