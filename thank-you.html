For the traveller

Duas for the journey

The Prophet ﷺ said these to people setting off on a journey. Say them for whoever is travelling.

أَسْتَوْدِعُ اللَّهَ دِينَكَ، وَأَمَانَتَكَ، وَخَوَاتِيمَ عَمَلِكَ

Astawdi‘ullāha dīnaka, wa amānataka, wa khawātīma ‘amalik

“I entrust to Allah your faith, your trust, and the last of your deeds.”

Abu Dawud 2600 · at-Tirmidhi 3443

زَوَّدَكَ اللَّهُ التَّقْوَى، وَغَفَرَ ذَنْبَكَ، وَيَسَّرَ لَكَ الْخَيْرَ حَيْثُمَا كُنْتَ

Zawwadakallāhut-taqwā, wa ghafara dhanbak, wa yassara lakal-khayra ḥaythumā kunt

“May Allah give you taqwa as your provision, forgive your sins, and make good easy for you wherever you are.”

at-Tirmidhi 3444
