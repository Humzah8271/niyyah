The journey

Your Umrah Journey

From entering Ihram and reciting the Talbiyah to Tawaf, Sa'ee and trimming your hair—a simple introduction to the steps of Umrah.

The Talbiyah

The Talbiyah is the pilgrim’s response to Allah’s call. It is recited after entering Ihram; we begin here before moving on to Tawaf and Sa'ee.

لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ

Labbayk Allāhumma labbayk, labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk, innal-ḥamda wan-ni‘mata laka wal-mulk, lā sharīka lak

“Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. All praise, all blessings and all sovereignty belong to You. You have no partner.”

Sahih al-Bukhari 1549 · Sahih Muslim 1184 · said once you have entered ihram

Tawaf

Rounds

Complete seven rounds around the Kaaba, keeping it on your left. Begin and finish each round in line with the Black Stone.

Seven rounds complete

رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ

Our Lord, accept from us. You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.

Quran 2:127A general dua for acceptance
Round 1 / 7

Sa'ee

Laps

Walk from Safa to Marwah, then back again. Each direction counts as one lap; seven laps finish at Marwah. Men jog between the green markers.

Seven laps complete

رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ

Our Lord, accept from us. You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.

Quran 2:127A general dua for acceptance
Lap 1 / 7 · Safa → Marwah · walking
  1. 01 · MIQAT

    Ihram

    Make your intention and enter ihram before crossing the miqat, the boundary for entering ihram. If you fly directly to Jeddah, prepare to do this on the plane.

  2. 02 · ×7

    Tawaf

    Walk around the Kaaba seven times, then pray two rak'ahs.

  3. 03 · ×7

    Sa'ee

    Seven laps between Safa and Marwah.

  4. 04 · DONE

    Shaving or trimming the hair

    Men shave or shorten their hair; women trim a small amount.

أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ

Remember Me; I will remember you. And thank Me, and never be ungrateful.Quran · Al-Baqarah 2:152

وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَارْغَبْ

Turning to your Lord alone with hope.Quran · Ash-Sharh 94:8
