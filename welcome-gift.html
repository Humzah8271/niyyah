Setting it as your wallpaper

iPhone

Tap Download, then open the image and tap the share button, then Save Image. Go to Settings, Wallpaper, Add New Wallpaper, and choose it from Photos.

Android

Tap Download, then open the image from your Downloads or Gallery. Tap the menu (three dots) and choose Set as wallpaper.
