Niyyah Umrah kits

We’re putting together Umrah kits with useful essentials in one bag. We’re checking samples first, and the kits are not available to order yet.

Our plans for the kits

What we’re working on

ESSENTIALS

Useful items for Umrah

We’re choosing the contents and checking samples. Each listing will show exactly what is included when the kits launch.

THE KIT

Packed by hand

We plan to assemble the kits ourselves, with personalised options for those who want them.

THE GUIDE

A step-by-step guide

We’re preparing an Umrah guide with Arabic duas, pronunciation help and English meanings. It will be reviewed by a qualified scholar before release.

THE PLAN

Help with planning

Planners and checklists to help you organise your trip and keep important details together.

FOR GROUPS

Families and mosques

We also plan to offer kits for families, mosques and travel groups.

ACROSS THE UK

UK delivery

Our kits are being developed for customers across the UK. Delivery details will be available when they launch.

Future travel services

Do you offer travel packages?

Not currently. Travel packages are a longer-term idea. We would only offer them with the required licences and financial protection in place, either ourselves or through a licensed operator. We are not taking travel bookings or deposits.
