Doing it properly

Protected, or not at all

In the UK, packages that include flights have to be sold with the right licences and financial protection, such as ATOL. We'll only offer packages once that's in place, either ourselves or with a licensed Umrah operator. Until then, we won't take deposits or bookings of any kind.
