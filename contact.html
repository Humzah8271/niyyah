Before you order

How do I order?

Everything is sold through our Etsy shop. Tap a product and it opens on Etsy, where you pay securely with Etsy's buyer protection.

Why is the price of a download higher on Etsy than I expected?

Etsy adds UK VAT to digital downloads at checkout. The prices on this website already include it, so they match what you pay.

How do downloads work?

As soon as payment goes through, your files appear on your Etsy Purchases page. Most come as an A4 PDF and a US Letter PDF. Print at home or at a print shop, as many copies as you need for your family.

How does personalisation work?

Type the name exactly as you want it in the personalisation box on Etsy. It's printed exactly as typed, so please check the spelling.

How long does delivery take?

Personalised items are made to order. Check the individual Etsy listing for current processing times, delivery destinations, charges and estimated arrival dates before ordering. Processing and delivery are separate stages.

Can I return something?

Our current personalised mug and tote listings offer returns and exchanges within 14 days. See the individual Etsy listing for the terms that apply to your purchase; we honour the policy shown when you order.

If an item is faulty, damaged or not as described, contact us through Etsy or at support@niyyahpackages.com with your order details. We will provide the remedy you are entitled to under consumer law. Your statutory rights are not affected.

For digital downloads, the 14-day cancellation right ends when supply begins only if you have expressly agreed to immediate supply and acknowledged that you will lose that right. Your rights if digital content is faulty or not as described still apply.

Is your Umrah guide checked by a scholar?

Our step-by-step Umrah guide is not yet available to buy. It will go on sale only after review by a qualified scholar. This planned review relates to the guide, not every product in our shop. Where scholarly opinions differ, seek guidance from your own qualified scholar or group leader.

Do you sell Umrah kits?

Not yet. We’re checking samples and preparing three kit options. You can ask about them using the contact form above.

Do you sell Umrah travel packages?

Not yet. It's something we'd love to offer in future, and only with the licences and protection UK law requires, such as ATOL. See our packages page.

Do you do bulk orders?

Yes, for personalised mugs, totes and printables. Send us a message above with your numbers and travel date.
