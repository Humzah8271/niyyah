Privacy notice

How Niyyah uses personal information when you visit this website or contact us.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Who is responsible?

Niyyah is operated by Humzah Ali Hamid, a sole trader based in Glasgow, Scotland. I am the controller of the personal information I use to run Niyyah. For privacy questions or requests, email support@niyyahpackages.com or message NiyyahPackages on Etsy.

Information we use and why

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, chosen topic, message and any order details you provide. We use these to answer you and handle your request. For an enquiry about buying from us, the legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract, or performing an existing contract. For other questions, our legitimate interest is providing helpful customer support.
  • Website operation: our hosting and content-delivery providers receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details and the files requested. This enables delivery and security of the site. Our legitimate interest is running a functioning, secure website.
  • Orders: purchases take place on Etsy. Etsy shares the order, contact, delivery and personalisation information needed to fulfil your purchase. We use it to perform the contract, meet accounting obligations and handle complaints or legal claims. Payment card details are handled by Etsy and its payment providers, not collected by this website.

Providing enquiry details is voluntary, but we need a way to contact you and enough information to answer. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or sensitive personal information through the contact form. We do not use this website to make automated decisions about you or build advertising profiles. Contacting us does not subscribe you to marketing.

Services that receive information

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and processes connection information. GitHub privacy statement.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) receives your contact-form fields and sends the message to our Gmail inbox. Google processes that email as our mailbox provider. You can instead contact us directly by email or through Etsy. FormSubmit privacy policy (PDF); Google privacy policy.
  • Porkbun provides our business-domain email service. Porkbun privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts used on the site. Cloudflare cdnjs supplies a library for the shop's 3D preview. Loading these resources sends connection information to those providers. Google privacy policy; Cloudflare privacy policy.

For purchases, information needed to fulfil an order may also be shared with Etsy, Printify and its production partners, delivery companies, and advisers or authorities where necessary. Our Etsy shop privacy policy explains our order handling. External shops and social-media sites apply their own privacy notices when you visit them. We do not sell personal information.

Processing outside the UK

These services can process information outside the UK, including in the United States. Their linked notices explain their locations and transfer arrangements. GitHub describes its use of standard contractual clauses and the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Google and Cloudflare describe their transfer mechanisms in their privacy notices. Porkbun describes safeguards including standard contractual clauses and adequacy decisions.

FormSubmit's published policy does not specify its processing countries or international-transfer safeguards. Those details are not yet verified. If you prefer not to send your message through FormSubmit, use our email address or Etsy Messages instead. Contact us for further information about providers and available transfer safeguards.

Cookies and browser storage

Our website code does not set analytics or advertising cookies, use marketing pixels, or save your form entries or journey counters in local storage. External resource providers still receive connection information when their resources load. FormSubmit states that its service may use cookies; see its policy above. Etsy and other sites you follow have their own cookie settings. We will update this notice if we add tracking or change how the site uses storage.

How long we keep information

We retain enquiries for as long as needed to answer them and handle related follow-up, complaints or claims, then delete them when no longer needed. The period depends on whether the enquiry results in an order or an unresolved issue. Tax and accounting records are kept for at least five years after the 31 January submission deadline for the relevant tax year, and longer where required by law or an unresolved matter. Our service providers apply their own retention rules to information they control, as explained in their notices.

Your choices and rights

You can request access to or correction of your information, deletion, restriction of use and, where applicable, a portable copy. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful use. Rights depend on the circumstances; records required by tax law cannot always be deleted.

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Explain your circumstances when contacting us so we can consider your objection.

Send requests to support@niyyahpackages.com. We normally respond within one month; if a lawful extension or identity check is needed, we will explain why. You can raise a concern with us or complain directly to the Information Commissioner's Office. You do not have to contact us first.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when our services or use of information changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
